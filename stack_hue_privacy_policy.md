# Privacy Policy for Stack Hue: Layer Block Match

*Last updated: September 17, 2026*

This Privacy Policy describes how **Stack Hue: Layer Block Match** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you play our mobile application.

### 1. Information We Do Not Collect
We are committed to player privacy:
- We do not require accounts, logins, or social registrations.
- We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, biometric data, or precise location information.
- We do not conduct cross-app user tracking or behavioral profiling.

### 2. Local Device Storage
All gameplay records—including highest level reached, best scores, earned coins, color theme preferences, sound toggles, and haptic feedback settings—are saved exclusively on your local device via standard browser/system `localStorage`. This data never leaves your device and can be erased at any time through the in-game Settings menu or by clearing the application storage.

### 3. Rewarded Video & Advertising Integrations
When you choose optional helper features (such as watching an ad to swap blocks, clear rows, or earn extra coins), industry-standard mobile ad networks (such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads) may process anonymous diagnostic device identifiers (such as IDFA or Google Advertising ID) solely for ad delivery, fraud mitigation, and frequency capping according to standard store policies.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Stack Hue does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under 13. The gameplay is non-violent, family-friendly, and suitable for all ages.

### 5. Third-Party Links
Our game runs fully offline and does not transmit data to external proprietary web servers. If you navigate to external links (e.g., store review pages or support channels), the privacy policies of those external services will govern.

### 6. Updates to This Policy
We may update this policy periodically to reflect platform updates or legal requirements. Updated versions will display a revised date at the top.
